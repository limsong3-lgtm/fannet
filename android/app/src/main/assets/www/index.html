Mobile apps · Gamma build · live records

One network, every pro sport

The Gamma build is a separate copy of the beta apps with expanded sports: combat sports as in beta, plus pro football, basketball and tennis. The bottom tabs stay as in beta; each sport changes what is inside them. Every app opens with a "view as" switcher and step list beside the phone, so you can tap around or jump straight to a flow.

Live database: in the Fan, Athlete, Manager, Promoter and Officials apps, Search reads real fighters, officials and events directly from the Fannet master database as you type (all 9 countries: Korea, China, Hong Kong, Uzbekistan, Taiwan, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Japan, Philippines, plus Vietnam). Open one and tap Details for the real fight history, scorecards, officials and fight cards. Read-only. Search shows only real records; the signed-in demo personas and flow screens are unchanged. Try "Pacquiao", "Inoue" or "Berwela".
