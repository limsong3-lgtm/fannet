Officials app, Gamma build

The Gamma build adds expanded sports: pro football and tennis officials now use the same app, alongside combat sports. Pick who you're viewing as. Daniel Price referees in the Pacific Pro League, which appoints him and holds his registration. Ines Duarte is a chair umpire certified by her national tennis federation and assigned by tournaments such as the Melbourne Indoor Open. Same tabs, same look; for them My bouts reads My matches. Karen's beta flow is unchanged: Karen Walsh is a VBO referee and judge. Commissions assign her to events and bouts from the Commission Desk (FMS), she answers here, and accepting opens a chat with the commission and the other officials on that event. There's no Openings tab: My bouts lists every bout she worked, and her licences live in Me. Tap around in the phone or jump to a step.

Gamma build: expanded sports (Lim, 7 Oct)
  • Same app, same tabs. Home, News, Assignments, My bouts and Chats keep their order and icons. For football and tennis officials the My bouts label reads My matches, the one wording swap allowed. Me stays behind the gear.
  • Who assigns. The Pacific Pro League's referees desk appoints Daniel's crews. Tournaments assign Ines, through her national federation. Accepting opens a crew chat with the organiser and the other officials.
  • Reports. Football: the referee files the match report (score, cautions, send-offs, incidents) with the league. Tennis: the chair umpire sends the scorecard and code violations to the tournament and the federation.
  • Privacy. Registration or certification numbers, expiry, date of birth, email and phone are visible only to the official's own league or federation. Organisers, clubs, players and fans see the name, role and that the official is registered or certified.
  • Assumed: app "today" stays 3 Oct 2026, so the 2026 league season and the November tennis events are ahead. The reports to file are from a pre-season cup final and an autumn tournament played on 2 Oct.
What Lim asked for (3 Oct)
  • Assignment notices from the Commission Desk. Later, the desk will send each assignment to the official's app, naming the event and the exact bouts and roles. Accepting or declining goes straight back to the desk.
  • Assignment chats. An accepted assignment opens a chat with the commission and the other officials on that event, as a group and one to one.
  • No Openings tab for officials. (The Licenses tab from the first pass is now in Me, and My bouts takes its place.)
Event report sign-off (Lim, 3 Oct)
  • Right after the event, once the supervisor or inspector closes it on the Commission Desk, each official gets the report for their own bouts as a push notice.
  • What each official checks: judges see their scorecard as entered, round by round. Referees see the result, the round and time, and the supervisor's note. Knockdowns, deductions, warnings and who stopped the bout aren't captured in scoring, so the supervisor writes them in the note (Lim). The ringside doctor checks the medical entries (injuries and suspensions).
  • Clear or Not right, per bout. "Not right" says what's different and goes straight to the inspector. Once every bout is clear, the sign-off shows on the Commission Desk.
  • Assumed: the report is final only when every official has cleared it, and officials have 48 hours to clear. The sign-off list shows who is still to clear.
Flags and corrections (Lim, 3 Oct)
  • Only events you worked. Every confirmed, reported or past assignment has "Fix event info" and "Whistleblower flag" at the bottom.
  • Fix event info goes to the commission: wrong or missing results, scorecards, fighter details, weights, rounds, officials, date or venue. It shows as Sent, then Fixed.
  • Whistleblower flag goes in confidence to the commission and to Fannet's integrity team (super admin), never to promoters, managers or fighters. Topics: approach to fix a result, betting, bribe or pressure, safety ignored, doping, misconduct.
  • Assumed: the official can hide their name from the commission, but super admin always sees it so they can follow up. A photo or file can be attached. My bouts lists everything sent and its status at the bottom.
My bouts and licences (Lim, 3 Oct)
  • Me (Lim, 3 Oct): no Officiating section (Home already shows it), and My flags and corrections moved to the bottom of My bouts. Edit changes every profile field, weight unit included. ID documents: local ID front and back, and passport photo page, with a warning when there is no passport. Notices go to the app and the registered email, so there are no notice settings. Log out sits at the bottom.
  • Availability is the first item in Assignments.
  • Licenses moved into Me (profile settings), with a summary there and a full screen for renewals, documents and applications.
  • My bouts replaces the Licenses tab. It lists the bouts she took part in, with chips for Missing info, Referee, Judge and Flagged.
  • Slide left on a bout for Fill in (missing data to the sanctioning commission, or Fannet super admin when no commission is listed, as with her 2011 and 2012 bouts) and Flag (critical issues: wrong result, fixing approach, betting, bribes, safety, doping, weights or gloves, hand wraps, misconduct). Tapping a bout opens the same options.
  • Search results too (Lim, 3 Oct): bouts in a fighter's fight history, an official's bouts and an event's fight card slide left for Edit (current data prefilled, change or fill in, sent to the sanctioning commission or super admin) and Flag. Everything sent is listed at the bottom of My bouts.
  • Your part in this bout (Lim, 3 Oct): Edit and Flag on a searched bout ask for the official's role in it (Referee, Judge, Inspector, Supervisor, Doctor, Promoter). Edit also has "My name is missing from this bout. Add me under this role."
Documents and seminars (Lim, 3 Oct)
  • Medical certificate and eye test are mandatory for every commission. Each commission decides whether it requires the others (first aid, police check). In the demo VBO requires both, Muay Thai Victoria makes them optional, and GAB requires a police check.
  • Seminar hours are set and credited by the commission on the Commission Desk (built in the Commission Desk thread). The official can register for seminars and see their hours, but can't add hours.
Event details (Lim, 4 Oct)
  • Posted as a pinned message, not a desk form. Most details aren't confirmed at sanctioning. When they are, the commission posts them in the officials group and pins the message. No extra step at the Commission Desk.
  • The Event details card shows the pinned message. Until there is one, it says the details are TBA. The pinned message also sits at the top of the officials group chat.
  • Late joiners see the whole chat. An official who accepts later, or replaces someone, sees every message posted before they joined, with a "joined the group" line. Accept Harbour Lights to see it.
  • Weigh-in is always the day before the event, so its date shows from the start. The weigh-in venue can be different from the event venue. Pacific Fight Night has its details pinned. Harbour Lights shows TBA.
Supervisor (Lim, 4 Oct)
  • One app for all officials (Lim, 4 Oct). Being a supervisor is an assignment, and it turns on a Supervisor card on Home (and a button on the assignment) with Officials, Weigh-in and Scoring.
  • Undercard supervisor (Lim, 4 Oct): the local commission names one supervisor who oversees all undercard bouts of the event.
  • Title bouts (Lim, 4 Oct): a global body (WBO, WBC, IBF, WBA) assigns the officials for its own title bouts, except the doctors, and can pick any official on file. When it picks you as supervisor, the Supervisor card covers that title bout only.
  • Officials: change any official on any bout at any time: before it starts, during it (a new judge scores from the current round; earlier rounds stay with the old judge), or after it ends as a record correction. Pick a licensed replacement and a reason.
  • Weigh-in: see every fighter's attempts against the limit, enter or edit weights, mark a miss with a note, then cancel the bout, agree a catchweight, or record the opponent accepting. Weights follow the Units setting.
  • Scoring: the desk's Supervisor scoring on a phone. Three judge cards, 10-must points with auto-fill, deductions, Next round and End match, the same warnings (points result with rounds missing, stoppage time 0:01 to 3:00, changing a past round, leaving a live bout), the public Supervisor note, and Reopen match.
  • White label: the About Me header shows the official's commission logo instead of Fannet. The shield is a placeholder for each commission's own logo.
Assumptions in this pass
  • Chat opens on accept. Before you accept, the assignment has no chat. Chats follow the same 72-hour rule as the other apps.
  • No messages to fighters, managers or promoters. Officials can only message the commission and other officials, to keep officiating independent.
  • Title bouts: the title owner (for example WBO) assigns the officials, and the home commission's desk passes on the notice.
  • Withdrawing is a request. You stay assigned until the commission finds a replacement.
  • Renewals need current documents (medical, eye test, first aid, police check, seminar hours). You can request a renewal early, and the commission approves it once the documents are in. No payment in the app for now (Lim, 3 Oct).
  • Availability: blocked dates are visible to the Commission Desk so it doesn't assign you on them.
  • Stand-alone account. An official can't add promoter, manager or fighter roles.
  • Shared with the other apps: Home layout standard (About Me, then Search, gear for Me), News, Search and W-L-D colors.
