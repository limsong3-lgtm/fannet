Manager app, Gamma build

The Gamma build adds expanded sports. Jordan Lee is now a multi-sport manager and player agent: his four fighters plus Kenta Mori (Pro Football, Harbour City FC) and Joel Reyes (Pro Basketball, Manila Tide). Offers for any of them come to Jordan, who accepts or declines on their behalf, asks promoters for fights, takes club enquiries for his players, and posts fighters and players to Openings. Pick a stable view, then tap around in the phone or jump to a step.

New in the Gamma build
  • One stable, many sports. Home groups the stable by sport, with a sport filter in the same chip style. The bottom nav is the beta nav: same tabs, order and icons.
  • Club offers go to the agent. Trial invites, transfer and loan enquiries and contract offers for Kenta and Joel come to Jordan, the same way promoters' offers for fighters do. The player sees an offer once Jordan accepts it. Your fee comes from the player's agent agreement (% of the playing contract).
  • No combat words on football and basketball screens: matches, games, rounds, clubs and the league table, not bouts, purses or divisions.
  • The league holds the record. Kenta's stats come from the Pacific Pro League and Joel's from the Pacific Basketball League. Fannet never decides who is a pro.
  • Privacy. Clubs see a player's public profile and the league's season stats only, never phone, email, date of birth or registration numbers; talks go through Jordan. Jordan sees the same about his players as beta shows him about his fighters (KYC and passport status).
  • Fan funding stays with the player. Kenta's season campaign runs from his own app, as fighters' campaigns do.
Rules carried over from the old mocks
  • Offers go to the manager. A promoter's offer for a linked fighter comes to the manager only. The fighter doesn't see it, and sees the bout once the manager accepts.
  • Fight requests aren't offers. The manager asks a promoter for a fight on the fighter's behalf. Limit: one request a month for the same fighter and promoter, whether the manager or the fighter sent it. A different promoter, or a different fighter, is fine.
  • Linking is mutual. Either side can ask, and every request must come with a written offer or conditions (Lim, 3 Oct): the manager states fee, length and what they'll do; the fighter states what they want. The link starts only when both accept. The commission registry is then updated. Unlinking also needs both sides, and only the fighter's home commission can unlink without consent.
  • Openings listings are posted by the manager for a linked fighter. Fighters without a manager post for themselves.
  • Fan funding stays with the fighter. The Fannet tab is in the fighter's app, not the manager's.
New in this pass
  • Passport flag (Lim, 4 Oct): in Openings, a fighter of yours with no passport on file is flagged on any fight or event whose venue is outside their country ("Fits Diego · no passport"). Putting them forward or requesting a fight shows a warning first, with Go ahead, Ask the fighter to add a passport, or Cancel. Warn only, as in the promoter app.
  • Both corners. When you manage both fighters in one bout (Marcus vs Liam), each corner gets its own offer and you answer each separately. The offer screen flags it.
  • KYC before accepting. You can't accept for a fighter whose KYC isn't finished (Hannah's offer shows this).
  • Your fee shows on each offer, from the fee in that fighter's management agreement.
  • Not legally binding notice on every offer and on interest in Openings, same as the fighter and promoter apps.
  • Title fights show the belt on the offer and on the booked bout.
  • Openings for managers shows which of your fighters fit each fight offer and event, lets you put a fighter forward, and lists fighters looking for a manager.
  • Home is about you. The header shows your photo, Fannet ID, licence and the combined record of your stable.
  • Home layout standard (all apps): About Me header, then a search bar for people, events and commissions. The gear at the top opens Me. No separate Me tab.
  • Shared with the other apps: News, Search, event chats with "+" for a new chat, W-L-D colors, and role switching in Me (promoter, manager and fighter on one account; officials and fans stand alone).
  • Passport status (Lim, 3 Oct): each fighter's page shows whether a passport is on file, and Home tags fighters without one. Without a passport, overseas fights are hard to get.
