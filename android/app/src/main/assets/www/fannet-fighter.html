Athlete app, Gamma build

The Gamma build turns the fighter app into the athlete app for every sport. It adds pro football, basketball and tennis, each with its own stats, fixtures or draws, Openings and Fannet campaigns, while combat sports work exactly as in beta: get an offer, accept it, then raise fan support. Pick who you're viewing as, then tap around in the phone or jump to a step.

Gamma build: expanded sports
  • One athlete app for every sport. The fighter app becomes the athlete app. The bottom bar keeps beta's five tabs, in the same order with the same icons; only what's inside each tab follows the sport.
  • Header per sport. Same layout as beta. The four tiles follow the sport: football Games, Goals, Assists, Minutes; basketball games and averages; tennis Ranking, Won, Lost, Titles. The strip under them shows the agent or manager, or "find one".
  • Who holds the record. The league (Pacific Pro League, Pacific Basketball League) or the player's national tennis federation holds the record and confirms who is a pro. Fannet never decides that.
  • Openings by sport. Football and basketball: trial invites, transfer and loan enquiries and contract offers, always through the agent when there is one. Tennis: tournament entries, wildcard requests, and exhibition and team-event invites.
  • Fannet per season or swing. Campaigns run per season (team sports) or per tournament swing (tennis), not per match. Fee: Fannet 20% + league or federation 5% + club or tournaments 5%.
  • Privacy. Email, phone, address, date of birth and registration details are visible only to the player and their own league or federation. Clubs, fans and other organizations never see them.
  • Sport words, not combat words. Matches, fixtures, games, tournaments and draws. News adds the player's sport to the tabs, with stories about them. All people, clubs, leagues and tournaments are made up. Rugby, volleyball, badminton, table tennis and golf come next.
How the two fighters differ
  • Rule of roles. Promoter, manager and fighter can be held by one account and switched between in Me (a role you don't hold yet shows "+" and starts its sign-up). Officials and fans are stand-alone accounts: they can't be added here, and an official or fan account can't hold any other role.
  • Mai has no manager. She sees and answers her own offers, and can ask promoters for fights.
  • Diego is managed by Jordan Lee. Offers go to Jordan only, and Jordan can accept or decline them without Diego seeing them, as the project description says. Diego sees a bout once it's agreed, and only Jordan can ask promoters for fights.
  • Link requests need written terms. A fighter asking a manager to link must write their conditions (fee they'll accept, length, what they want). A manager's request comes with a written offer. Not binding until a management agreement is signed.
  • Unlinking is mutual. Either side can propose it, and both must agree. Only the commission can unlink without consent.
What changed from the old mocks
  • News is new. Combat sports stories are clipped automatically from news sites. It's its own tab in the bottom bar, filtered to your sports, and keeps loading older stories as you scroll down. Pull down at the top to refresh with new stories. Press and drag the tab row (My feed, All sports, each sport) left or right to see the ones off screen; a fade shows there's more. Stories show as Compact (thumbnail and headline) or Expanded (big image, summary, Read and Share buttons). Share sends a link out to other apps only, not between Fannet users. The gear on the News tab sets My feed: sports, type of story (results, announcements, rankings, rules, interviews, promotion news) and region or country. Each shows the headline, source, time and a short summary, and links out to the full story. The headlines here are made-up samples.
  • Search is in every app, as a search bar right under the About Me header on Home. The gear at the top of Home opens Me (profile, roles, settings). It finds people (fighters, promoters, managers, officials), events and commissions. Details opens a full-screen history (a fighter's every bout with result, method, opponent record and event; a promoter's events; an event's fight card; a commission's recent events). Tap any bout in a fighter's history to expand it (one at a time; opening another closes the last): commission, promoter, matchmaker, all officials (referee, judges, timekeeper, doctor, inspector) and each judge's scorecard. For stoppages it shows the time and the cards at the stoppage. Forward shares a link to the result in another app (messages, email, copy link, the phone's share menu). It doesn't send between Fannet users.
  • Chats open into a chat window with the running history and a reply box. Every chat belongs to one event and you can reply until 72 hours after it. After that it's read only for 7 more days, then deleted.
  • W-L-D records use one color standard across FMS and every Fannet app: wins green, losses red, draws blue.
  • Fannet branding replaces Ringside.
  • Events are part of Openings. Promoters choose whether each event appears in Openings; those that do show under Events with Request a fight.
  • Fannet (Fan Network Funding) is the old Funds tab, renamed so the name explains itself. The first time a fighter opens it, they must accept the service agreement: 30% service fee (5% to the commission, 5% to the promoter, 20% to Fannet), if the fight is off, each fan chooses in the app between Fannet credit and a refund (processing fee not refunded), recipients handle their own taxes, and it's an optional, voluntary product.
  • Openings replaces the old Bouts tab. Fighters see fight offers posted by promoters and matchmakers, and managers looking to link. Filter by All, Fight offers or Manager links; the gear narrows by minimum purse, rounds and region or country. Managed fighters' interest goes to their manager.
  • Bouts live on Home, right under the header in a bordered "My Upcoming Bouts" section: upcoming bouts with their sanction and acceptance checks, then recent results. The Bouts tab is gone.
  • Home is about the fighter. The top shows their photo, ring name, Fannet ID, full W-L-D record with KOs, and their manager.
  • Promises, not prizes. The old setup allowed up to 5 prizes. Now the fighter must offer at least 3 promises, up to 5.
  • Anonymous support is an option for fans. The old progress screen showed every supporter's email.
  • ID documents (Lim, 3 Oct): Me has local ID front and back and the passport photo page. With no passport, the fighter is warned that fights overseas will be hard to get, and a managed fighter is told their manager can see it. View as Diego to see it.
