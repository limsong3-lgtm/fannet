One app for players, captains get extra buttons

This is what members of Southbank Sunday FC use. A captain is not a separate app: when the organizer makes a member Captain, their Player app shows captain tools for that game or the season.

    How the captain role works

    • The organizer sets it in the control panel under Members, for one game or the whole season.
    • Captain tools: pick the side from who said they're coming, send the team sheet, and confirm the score with the other club or the league.
    • Captains see teammates' names and availability only. Contact details stay with the club.
    • Bottom tabs are the beta athlete app's: Home, News, Openings, Fannet, Chats.

    All people and clubs are made up. Same club and data as the club control panel.
