    Rules in this mock
      At scale
      • Why there's no open search. Registers will hold thousands of clubs and players. A browsable search would let anyone list them, see who is on file and pick a record to impersonate, and it would leak private details. So sign-up only matches, in this order: an invite; otherwise the person picks their home commission, league or federation from the short public list of bodies (never people) and the verified ID is matched inside that body only; if their body isn't listed, the profile is matched across every body on Fannet for the sport. Never a list of people at any step.
      • Anti-abuse. Exact matches only, never partial lists. Attempts are rate-limited, and every claim and link is logged for the body, which can undo it.
