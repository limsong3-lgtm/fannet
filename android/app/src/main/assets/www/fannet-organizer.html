Fannet Organizer app · Gamma build mock-up

Start a club, invite your players

Built for someone who has never used Fannet: a parent running a kids' club or a captain with a group chat. Sign-up, the club's control panel, its website and the first invites take about 10 minutes, one question per screen.

    What the organizer gets

    • Control panel for members, sessions, results and invites, set up from a template for the sport.
    • Club website made from the control panel, live at once and marked "Not yet verified" until confirmed.
    • Invite links: one club link for anyone (the club approves) and personal links for people with an email or phone on file (they join straight away after the ID check).
    • The bottom tabs stay as in the beta promoter app: Home, News, Events, Openings, Chats.
