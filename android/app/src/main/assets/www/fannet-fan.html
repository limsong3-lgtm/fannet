Fan app, Gamma build

Gamma build, with expanded sports. Pro football, basketball and tennis join the combat sports: fans follow clubs and players, see fixtures, league tables, tennis draws and rankings, read news for each sport, and back a player's season or tour campaign. Everything from beta works as before. How fans support fighters through Fan Network Funding. A fighter starts a funding campaign and shares the link on social media. A fan who taps it is encouraged to get the Fannet app, or can contribute once without it. Pick who you're viewing as, then tap around in the phone or jump to a step.

Rules in this mock
  • Funding campaigns (Lim). A fighter can start one once the promoter's event is sanctioned and both fighters agreed to the bout. The fighter sets the promises and shares the campaign link on social media.
  • From the link (Lim). A fan who taps the link either contributes once, or is invited to get the app and go through on-boarding.
  • The app comes first (Lim). The link page gives Lim's three reasons: choosing credit or refund in the app if a fight is cancelled, an ID check only if needed and only once, and following fighters with news.
  • Credit or refund, chosen in the app (Lim). If a fight is cancelled or the fighter pulls out, the fan chooses in the app: Fannet credit for the full amount, or a refund of the contribution to their card; the processing fee paid at checkout isn't refunded. Sam has one waiting on Home and in the Fannet tab. The fee shown is a sample (1.7% + 30c). Credit kept for a fighter's next fight waits for the fan to say yes (Claude's default).
  • ID checks for contributions (Lim). Some countries require KYC even for donors. App users do it once. A one-off contributor gives their country and adds an ID where it's required. In this mock the Philippines and Thailand are sample countries that require it.
  • One-off without the app (Claude's default). Name, email and country for the receipt; any credit is kept under that email and used in the app.
  • In the app (Claude's default). Fans see campaigns from fighters they follow, on the fighter's page and from links shared with them. There is no list of every campaign.
  • Promises are a thank-you, not an auction (Lim). The biggest supporter gets promise #1, the second biggest #2, and so on, by total given to that campaign.
  • Giving again to move up. Repeat contributions add to the fan's total. The campaign stays open and places can change until the end of fight day (Lim), then the list locks. Ties go to whoever reached the total first (Claude's default). No "you've been passed" alerts. Not shown in the phone: one card can pay only a few times in a short window (card testing), the KYC threshold counts the running total, a chargeback comes off the total, promises are delivered after payout, the fighter's own team can't contribute, and the fighter sees each fan's messages grouped.
  • Support, not a bet (Lim). The fighter gets the money win, lose or draw. Fans don't pick winners.
  • Fan Network Funding terms: money is held until the event, then paid out; 30% service fee (20% Fannet, 5% commission, 5% promoter) comes out of what the fighter receives.
  • No ID at fan sign-up (Lim). A fan adds an ID only if the country they live in requires an ID check for contributions, and only once, in Me. Linh lives in the Philippines (a sample KYC country), so view as Linh to see the block.
  • Built as if payments work (Lim, 3 Oct). The real payment system is linked once the rest is built.
  • $5 minimum, no maximum (Lim).
  • Payout disclaimer (Lim). Wherever the fighter's share is shown: the final amount credited to the athlete's account depends on financial service fees and exchange rates, and can only be calculated at the time of deposit. Amounts raised and the fighter's share are labelled as estimates (Lim).
  • Simplified markup (Lim). A flat payment processing fee is added at checkout instead of working out card surcharges live: "Contribution to Mai $100.00, Payment processing fee $5.50, Total charge $105.50". The mock uses 5.5% (Lim said 5% to 6%). It isn't refunded, and paying with Fannet credit has no fee (Claude's default).
  • Name, pseudonym or Anonymous (Lim) on the supporter list, chosen each time. Sam uses "Southside Sam".
  • One message per contribution (Lim), shown on the receipt. There is no chat with fighters.
  • Fan accounts are fan only (Lim). Fighters, promoters and managers sign up with a different email.
  • Match Up (Lim asked for it in Search). Choose a sport on a slider (Pro Boxing, Pro Muay, Pro Kun Khmer, Pro KickBoxing, MMA; Lim), then pick any two fighters in it, booked or not, with search by name or country and a weight class slider in the picker (the mock has about 50 sample fighters; the app searches everyone on Fannet), to see the Fannet Prediction (from records and KO rates, shown once both have 4 or more bouts; same formula as the promoter app), a tale of the tape with the last 5 fights as green, red and blue boxes (Lim), whether the bout is booked, and Forward. It's a fan view with no money involved, and fans don't pick or vote on winners (Claude's defaults).
  • No chats for fans. Chats are for the people working an event.
  • Gamma: expanded sports (Lim, 7 Oct). Pro Football (Pacific Pro League), Pro Basketball (Pacific Basketball League) and Pro Tennis (each player's national tennis federation) sit beside the combat sports in sports choices, News, Events, Search, Following and Fannet. The bottom nav is the same as beta. All clubs, people, leagues and tournaments are fictional. Fans see public info only: contact details, licence or registration numbers and dates of birth stay with the player's league or federation. Campaigns are per season or per tennis swing; the fee split is Fannet 20%, league or federation 5%, club or tournament 5%. The league or federation confirms who is a pro, not Fannet. Match Up stays combat only. Rugby, volleyball, badminton, table tennis and golf come next, the same way.
